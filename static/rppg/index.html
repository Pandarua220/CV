Measure your heart rate with your camera

Every heartbeat changes the blood flow in the vessels of your face, which slightly changes the intensity of the light reflected by your skin. By capturing these changes with your camera, we calculate your heart rate. This technique is called remote photoplethysmography (rPPG). The demo runs the rPPG algorithms used in Prof. Wenjin Wang's lab at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where I did my undergraduate research, entirely in your browser. For details, see Algorithmic Principles of Remote PPG (Wang et al., IEEE TBME, 2017).

Your video never leaves this device. All processing happens locally in the browser, and nothing is uploaded or stored.
